How the measurement works

Left border = inner left − outer left. Right border = outer right − inner right. Left percentage = left ÷ (left + right) × 100. Top and bottom use the same calculation. The worse axis sets the centering estimate.

Photograph the card flat and square to the camera. Correct perspective before aligning the design. The displayed decimal is a calculation resolution, not a guarantee of photographic accuracy.

Beyond centering

Inspect all four corners for rounding and whitening, edges for chips and wear, and the surface for scratches, print lines, dents and staining. This tool does not assess those features or guarantee a slab grade.
